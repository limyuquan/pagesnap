Click the Page Snap icon in the toolbar.

The whole page, not just the screen.

It scrolls and stitches for you, one screen at a time, then puts the page back the way you left it. Press Esc to stop early and keep what it has.

  • Sticky headers show up once, not on every screen.
  • Chat bubbles and cookie bars stay where they belong.
  • Lazy images load before their screen is taken.
  • Apps that scroll in a panel are expanded to their full length.

Opens ready to draw.

The arrow tool is already armed. Box, underline, highlighter, pen and text are one key away, and any mark can be clicked to move, resize, rotate or bend it.

Sized for the model that reads it.

⌘C copies an image AI chats take in at full resolution. On a very tall page it copies up to 2,576 px around your marks, so nothing gets shrunk into a blur.

2,576 px

Stays on your machine.

No account, no uploads, no network requests. Captures live in your browser’s storage and nowhere else, and Chrome shows no warning when you install it.

Never asks for access to all sites, your tabs or history, downloads or clipboard permissions.

Install it.

  1. Download

    Get the .zip from the latest release and unzip it somewhere it can stay.

  2. Load

    Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.

  3. Pin

    Pin Page Snap to the toolbar. Click it on any page, or press Alt Shift P.
