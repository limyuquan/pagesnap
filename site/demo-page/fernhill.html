Questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply right away and we credit what is left of your current plan. Downgrades start at your next renewal.

What happens to my notes if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. Your account moves to Personal, and notebooks beyond the first three become read only until you upgrade again.

Do you offer refunds?

Yearly plans can be refunded within 14 days of purchase. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time.

Is there a discount for students?

Students and teachers get Plus for half price with a school email address. It renews every year while you are enrolled.

Where is my data stored?

Notes are encrypted on your device before they sync. You can choose a data region when you create a Team.

How do guest editors work?

Invite anyone to a shared notebook by email. Guests can edit that notebook only and do not count as members.

Bring your notes home

Import from text files, Markdown folders and most notes apps.
